That can be faster than a call centre, but the “request → accepted offer” time also adds to waiting time. Because the app already sends status notifications and tracks routes, these timestamps exist and a pilot can measure from them (see the measurement plan). An optional “auto-assign the nearest suitable truck” mode is the option with the largest gain in the simulation.Çekicin arbeitet derzeit nach einem Angebot-und-Verhandlungs-Modell, nicht nach automatischer Zuweisung.
